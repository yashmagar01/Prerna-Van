# Prerna Tirth --- Values, Identity & Organizational Understanding

**Document:** `VALUES.md`\
**Purpose:** Capture the working understanding of Prerna Tirth's
identity, philosophy, community model, physical setting and public
expression.\
**Audience:** Website, design, content, technical and community teams.\
**Status:** Working organizational narrative based on collected

## 1. Executive understanding

Prerna Tirth (प्रेरणा तीर्थ) is described in the collected documentation as
an open, community-driven initiative centered on inspiration,
connection, nature, mindfulness, learning and personal growth. Its
guiding expression is:

> **Inspire. Connect. Transform.**

Its associated Marathi mantra is:

> **"छान छान मी क्षणोक्षणी प्रत्येक क्षणी 🌿"**

The philosophy document translates this as: *"Beautiful am I, in every
moment, at every second."*

Maitri Hills, near Kadus and Rajgurunagar in Pune district, is
identified as the physical setting associated with the initiative. The
documentation distinguishes Prerna Tirth's broader philosophy and
community identity from Maitri Hills' physical facilities and
agro-tourism setting.

This distinction is central to understanding the initiative: the place
supports the experiences, while Prerna Tirth's identity is expressed
through the meaning, learning and connection associated with those
experiences.

**Important:** This document summarizes the supplied working
documentation. It does not independently verify legal status, formal
governance, official mission wording, current leadership, current
facilities or every claim made in internal analysis.

## 2. What is Prerna Tirth?

The collected material presents Prerna Tirth as a community movement
rather than simply a commercial destination. It aims to bring people
together through nature, shared experiences, reflection, learning and
human connection.

Its public-facing identity combines: - A philosophy of being present and
finding beauty in each moment. - Opportunities for people to connect
with one another and the natural environment. - Learning and community
experiences, including youth-oriented gatherings. - Recreational
activities that can create shared memories and a sense of belonging. -
An invitation to participate in a welcoming community.

The Organization Hub includes the phrases **"Join the Journey," "DM to
Join" and "Free for All."** These indicate an open and accessible
community positioning in the collected material. The exact joining
process and whether every activity is free must be confirmed before the
website makes an unqualified promise.

## 3. Guiding philosophy: Inspire, Connect, Transform

### 3.1 Inspire --- प्रेरणा

Inspiration is the starting point. The documentation describes stepping
away from the pressures and isolation of everyday urban life and
encountering nature, reflection and new perspectives.

For the website, this means communicating possibility and curiosity
rather than promising instant personal change. Inspiration may be
expressed through the landscape, meaningful ideas, learning experiences
and stories from the community.

### 3.2 Connect --- जोडणे

Connection is the lived, social part of the experience. Shared meals,
open conversations, eco-tours, group activities and learning sessions
provide ways for people to interact with one another and with their
surroundings.

For the website, connection should be represented through real people,
candid community moments, inclusive language and clear ways to
participate.

### 3.3 Transform --- परिवर्तन

Transformation is presented as the hoped-for outcome: reflection,
learning, a mental reset and self-evolution. The collected philosophy
document frames the experience as more than a getaway.

For the website, transformation should be expressed as an invitation or
aspiration, not a guaranteed therapeutic or psychological result. Use
grounded descriptions of learning and experience rather than unsupported
promises.

### How the three pillars relate

The three pillars form a narrative: 1. **Inspire:** encounter an idea or
experience that opens a new perspective. 2. **Connect:** engage with
people, place and shared activities. 3. **Transform:** take away
reflection, learning or a renewed sense of direction.

This is a conceptual framework drawn from the supplied philosophy
document, not a claim that every visitor experiences the same sequence
or outcome.

## 4. The mantra: presence and self-acceptance

> **"छान छान मी क्षणोक्षणी प्रत्येक क्षणी 🌿"**

The documentation associates this mantra with two themes:

-   **Presence:** Paying attention to the current moment rather than
    remaining absorbed in constant comparison or pressure.
-   **Self-acceptance:** Approaching oneself and each moment with
    appreciation.

The mantra is an important expression of the initiative's identity. On
the website, it should be presented with care and correct Devanagari
rendering, not used merely as a decorative slogan.

The English translation in the source documentation is an interpretation
rather than a word-for-word linguistic analysis. Confirm preferred
spelling, punctuation, translation and usage with the organization
before final publication.

## 5. Nature and Maitri Hills

Maitri Hills is described as the physical setting associated with Prerna
Tirth.

The Operations document gives the location as: - At/Post Kadus, Taluka
Khed - Near Rajgurunagar - Pune district, Maharashtra 412404 - In the
foothills of the Sahyadri mountain range

The document describes the site as operating in an agro-tourism context
and lists: - Vegetarian rural food - Agri-walks and eco-tours - A
nursery - Swimming pool and boating - Indoor and outdoor sports -
Tractor rides

These details come from the collected Operations document and should be
reconfirmed for current availability, accuracy, naming and publication.

### How the relationship should be communicated

-   **Prerna Tirth** is the initiative's philosophy, community and
    overarching experience.
-   **Maitri Hills** is the associated physical location and its
    facilities.
-   The website should explain their relationship clearly without
    presenting them as interchangeable names or implying an unconfirmed
    legal or operational structure.

## 6. Learning, youth and community

The Reel Study document identifies both educational/reflective content
and joyful community experiences as major themes in the existing
social-media material.

### Learning and reflection

The documentation associates Dr. Ramlal Katariya with teachings around
the mantra and broader ideas of grounded living and mindfulness. It also
describes the "सोच" youth camp held at Maitri Hills on 9--11 May 2025.

The supplied record describes sessions addressing topics such as: -
Constitutional awareness - Rational thinking - Astronomy and astrology -
Gender equality - Relationships and handling rejection - Mental health
and youth - Thoughtful use of social media - Cultural expression and
social issues

It states that the camp was associated with the Maharashtra
Andhashraddha Nirmoolan Samiti and that writer Arvind Jagtap inaugurated
it. The document reports 120 participants from different regions of
Maharashtra.

These are historical details from the collected media study, not
independently verified current organizational affiliations. Confirm
event names, dates, partners, speakers, attendance and permissions
before publishing an official event archive.

### Joy and shared experiences

The media study also describes videos showing group enjoyment,
recreation, nature activities and candid interactions. In its
interpretation, these moments demonstrate the community side of the
philosophy.

For the website, learning and joy should coexist. The site should not
portray the initiative as exclusively solemn or exclusively
recreational.

## 7. Working values inferred from the documentation

The following values are a synthesis of the supplied material. They are
useful for design and content decisions, but should be validated with
the organization rather than treated as a formally approved values
charter.

### Presence

Make room for attention, reflection and appreciation of the current
moment.

### Human connection

Value meaningful interaction, shared experiences and community
participation.

### Openness and accessibility

Present the initiative as welcoming and open to participation,
consistent with the documented "Join the Journey" and "Free for All"
language. Confirm the practical terms of participation.

### Learning and thoughtful inquiry

Make space for knowledge, discussion, youth development and considered
engagement with social issues, as represented in the documented camp and
educational content.

### Kindness and compassion

The Reel Study records content emphasizing that care and small acts of
kindness matter. This theme can inform the tone of the website without
claiming that it is a formally adopted organizational policy.

### Nature and ecological connection

Treat the rural landscape, agricultural setting and ecological
experiences as meaningful parts of the initiative's context.

### Joy and belonging

Represent recreation, shared laughter and collective experiences as
legitimate parts of community life.

### Self-reflection and growth

Invite people to learn and reflect without promising uniform outcomes or
presenting the website as a substitute for professional support.

## 9. Principles for representing Prerna Tirth on the website

### Tell the story before listing the facilities

Help visitors understand the initiative's purpose before presenting the
location as a collection of amenities.

### Show real people and real experiences

Prioritize approved, authentic photographs and event stories. Avoid
presenting stock or generated imagery as actual members or events.

### Balance reflection with joy

The site should make space for philosophy and learning as well as
recreation and celebration.

### Keep Marathi central where appropriate

Preserve the mantra accurately and make Devanagari a considered part of
the visual and content system. Confirm the final language strategy with
stakeholders.

### Use welcoming, non-exclusive language

Explain how people can learn more or participate. Do not invent
eligibility criteria, membership benefits or guarantees.

### Communicate the location accurately

Distinguish the community initiative from the physical facilities of
Maitri Hills, and confirm operational details before publication.

### Make only supportable claims

Avoid unsupported statements about mental-health benefits, measurable
transformation, organizational registration, impact, leadership or
partnerships.

## 10. What Prerna Tirth is not established to be

The current documentation does not establish that Prerna Tirth is: - A
registered nonprofit, charity or other specific legal entity. - A
clinical mental-health or therapeutic service. - A formal religious
institution. - A paid membership organization or a commercial booking
platform. - A guaranteed personal-transformation program.

These are not declarations that such descriptions are false; they are
boundaries on what the collected documentation currently supports.
Confirm formal status and intended public description with the
organization.

## 12. How this document should guide the website

This document should help the team make content and design decisions
consistently.

When deciding whether a section belongs on the site, ask: 1. Does it
help visitors understand Prerna Tirth? 2. Does it express inspiration,
connection, learning, nature, joy or reflection? 3. Is the information
accurate and approved? 4. Does it help visitors take a useful next step?
5. Can it be communicated clearly without exaggerated claims?

The website should aim to leave visitors with a grounded understanding
of the initiative and a clear way to explore or connect with it.
