# ⛰️ Maitri Hills Operations (The Base)

> [!summary] Physical Anchor
> The digital and spiritual entity of [[Prerna Tirth Philosophy|Prerna Tirth]] is physically hosted at **Maitri Hills Agri Tourism Centre / Maitri Hills**.

## 📍 Geographic Location
- **Address:** At/Post Kadus, Taluka-Khed (Near Rajgurunagar)
- **District:** Pune, Maharashtra 412404
- **Distance:** Located approximately 51–53 kms from Pune and 140 kms from Mumbai.
- **Topography:** Nestled in the evergreen foothills of the Sahyadri mountain range.

## 👥 Key Personnel (Core Network)
- **Dr. Ramlal Katariya** (Listed as Farmer / Core Site Owner in agri-tourism registries)
- **Sachin Chavan** (Historically associated with grounds operations/promotion)

## 🌾 Facilities & Offerings
The site is built around the ATDC (Agri Tourism Development Corporation) model, facilitating community bonding through natural immersion.
- **Food Pipeline:** 100% Pure Vegetarian rural food.
- **Ecological Immersion:** Agri-walks, Eco-Tours, Nursery.
- **Activities:** Swimming pool, boating, indoor/outdoor sports, tractor rides.

## ⚙️ Strategic Separation
While *Maitri Hills* acts as the registered tourism space/commercial ground, outfacing operations for the group are channeled through the *Prerna Tirth* brand, combining agro-tourism infrastructure with a zero-cost, communal mindfulness collective.
